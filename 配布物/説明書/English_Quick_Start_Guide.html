1. Get Sound in 60 Seconds (Choose A or B)

[A] Standalone App (OBS / VRChat / Discord / Recording — No DAW Needed)

  1. Recommended: Run the bundled all-in-one installer at the root of this ZIP folder (VocalGzzio_Setup_v5.4.1.exe, or VocalGzzio_Trial_Setup_v5.4.1.exe for the Free Trial). It installs the Standalone App, VST3, and WHQL-signed WDM Virtual Driver automatically. (If Windows SmartScreen prompts "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.)
    (For portable/manual launch, open VocalGzzio.exe or VocalGzzio Trial.exe inside 単体起動アプリ, where VocalGzzioWdmInstaller.exe is also included.)
  2. Click 🎤 うた自動 (Auto-Setup: Sing) at the top bar and sing for 5 seconds.
  3. In OBS, Discord, or VRChat microphone settings, select:
    Line (VocalGzzio Driver (WDM))
  4. Using hardware direct monitoring on your audio interface? Turn ON 🎧 自分の耳への返しミュート (Mute Self-Monitor) so you don't hear a doubled voice while OBS still receives the full processed audio.

[B] VST3 / AU Plugin inside a DAW (REAPER / Cubase / Studio One / FL / Ableton)

  1. Run the bundled .exe installer at the root of this folder, or manually copy VocalGzzio.vst3 (VocalGzzio Trial.vst3 in Free Trial) from the VST3プラグイン folder into:
    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
    (On macOS, run the .pkg installer for AU, VST3 & Standalone).
  2. Restart your DAW, insert VocalGzzio on your vocal track, and click 🎤 うた自動.

3. Troubleshooting

  • Other apps (VRChat / Discord / Browser) lose sound on Windows: Click 🛠 cluster・通話 音消え一発修復 or 🌐 全アプリ共存・共有モード at the top of the screen.
  • Loud buzzing noise on Yamaha AG03 / AG06 when switching apps: Set sample rate to 48000 Hz and buffer size to 192 or 256 samples in Yamaha Steinberg USB Driver Control Panel, or read our full English guide: Fixing the Yamaha AG03/AG06 Buzz Bug.
